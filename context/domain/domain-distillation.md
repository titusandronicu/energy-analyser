# Domain distillation: Energy Analyser

## Summary

Energy Analyser is a single-owner web app that answers two questions for the owner of a home solar system: "what should the battery do today?" and "was recent usage normal?" (`README.md:3`). A home lab (an external system) computes the data, writes the advice text and pushes both to the app. The app signs the owner in, stores the pushes, applies its own season-aware rules and shows verdicts in plain Polish. The Core is two subdomains: **advice and usage verdicts** (recommendation, usage insight, norm, verdict) and **cost foresight** (bill forecast against the real invoice); the product goals name both (`context/foundation/prd-v3.md:19`, `:44`, `:49`). Of 15 Core and 28 Supporting terms, 5 are missing in code; all 5 are documented as planned, proposed or blocked, so none is a surprise. The strongest drift is not a missing word but overloaded ones: "complete" carries five different rules, "norm" two computations and "state" six meanings. The #1 candidate is the **push (ingest) boundary**: its rules are split across a zod contract, a service and an SQL function that five migrations redefine, a direct RPC bypasses the contract, and anonymous callers get validation details before a token is checked. No documented rule is ignored or contradicted by the code; 14 of 35 rules are only declared (soft, client-only or held by absence), 19 are enforced and 1 exposure is undocumented. Drift: 14 rows, by fix side 3 code, 8 docs, 3 decision. 5 open questions have an owner.
