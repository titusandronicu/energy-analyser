## Notes

Roadmap open question 4 (raised after S-14): the "Co to znaczy?" section on the usage card explains words, not numbers. The owner asked (2026-09-26) to replace it with the meaning of the day — the norm computed as a median over the period, and what counts as low, normal, high and very high consumption in kWh — derived from the home's own history. Public Polish household figures (a ~140 m² house with a heat pump) are to be used where own data is missing, and the norm should correct itself over time.

Outcome: the public figure is shown only as a comparison line and never enters the norm, because this house uses about 2.8 times the estimate (decisions.md 2026-09-26); the norm corrects itself from the home's own median. Showing estimated ranges when own data is short was confirmed by the owner during the implementation review and is queued as a separate change (`follow-ups/review-fixes.md`, item 1).
